# Semicircle Command

> Source: `code/docs/geogebra-manual-main-en/modules/ROOT/pages/commands/Semicircle.adoc`
> Page slug: `commands/Semicircle`

## Syntax

`Semicircle( <Point>, <Point> )`

Creates a semicircle above the segment between the two points and displays its length in *Algebra View*.
