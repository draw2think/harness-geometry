# CircularArc Command

> Source: `code/docs/geogebra-manual-main-en/modules/ROOT/pages/commands/CircularArc.adoc`
> Page slug: `commands/CircularArc`

## Syntax

`CircularArc( <Midpoint>, <Point A>, <Point B> )`

Creates a circular arc with midpoint between the two points.

> **Note**
>
> - The arc length is displayed in *Algebra View*.
> - Point *B* does not have to lie on the arc.
> - See also ![Mode circlearc3](../../../../code/docs/geogebra-manual-main-en/modules/ROOT/assets/images/24px-Mode_circlearc3.svg.png) [Circular Arc](../../../../code/docs/geogebra-manual-main-en/modules/ROOT/pages/tools/Circular_Arc.adoc) tool.
